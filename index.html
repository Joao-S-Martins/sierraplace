Sierra Place Apartments in Porterville, CA

1 & 2 Bedroom Apartments Near Sierra View Medical Center

Sierra Place is a garden-style apartment community in central Porterville, California, with one- and two-bedroom apartments in four floor plans. Cool off in the enclosed swimming pool, park under covered parking and come home to central air conditioning. We're 0.7 miles from Sierra View Medical Center and less than a mile from Highway 65.

Our community is tucked into a quiet neighborhood setting with landscaped grounds, yet it's close to shopping, dining and downtown Porterville. Homes feature large bedrooms with plenty of natural light and ample closet and storage space, and select homes have a private patio or balcony. On-site laundry facilities are available, and small to medium pets are welcome.

Why renters choose Sierra Place

  • A short commute. Porterville Developmental Center, the Walmart Distribution Center, Eagle Mountain Casino and Porterville College are all within about 15 minutes. See commute times.
  • Close to the hospital. Sierra View Medical Center is about a 15-minute walk or a 3-minute drive. Apartments near Sierra View Medical Center.
  • Room for roommates. The two-bedroom, two-bath Redwood and the two-story Sequoia give everyone their own space. Compare floor plans.
  • Moving from out of town? Porterville is about an hour from Bakersfield and 40 minutes from Visalia. Read our moving guide.

Porterville sits in Tulare County at the foot of the southern Sierra Nevada, between Bakersfield and Fresno, and is a short drive from Lake Success, Sequoia National Forest and Sequoia & Kings Canyon National Parks.
