Frequently Asked Questions

Sierra Place Apartments, Porterville, CA

Answers to common questions about living at Sierra Place. Don't see yours? Call (559) 781-8000, Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 4 PM.

Where is Sierra Place Apartments?

Sierra Place is at 333 N. Indiana Street, Porterville, CA 93257, in central Porterville. It's 0.7 miles from Sierra View Medical Center, about 5 minutes from downtown Main Street and less than a mile from Highway 65. See the neighborhood and commute times.

What size apartments do you have?

We have one- and two-bedroom apartments in four floor plans: the Cedar (1 bedroom, 1 bath, about 654 sq ft), the Sierra (2 bedrooms, 1 bath, about 859 sq ft), the Sequoia (2 bedrooms, 1.5 baths, about 1,110 sq ft) and the Redwood (2 bedrooms, 2 baths, about 1,133 sq ft). See the floor plans.

How much is rent?

Rent depends on the floor plan and what's available. Call Homes for Rent at (559) 781-8000, Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 4 PM, for current pricing and availability.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Small to medium pets are welcome. Large dogs are not accepted. Ask our office about pet limits and deposits.

Do the apartments have a washer and dryer?

Most apartments do not include a washer and dryer. On-site laundry facilities are available to all residents.

Is there a swimming pool?

Yes. Sierra Place has an enclosed swimming pool with a sundeck, surrounded by landscaped grounds.

Is there a fitness center?

No. Sierra Place does not have a fitness center.

Is parking covered?

Covered parking is available.

Do the apartments have air conditioning?

Yes. Apartments have central air conditioning.

What are your office hours?

Sierra Place is leased and managed by Homes for Rent. The office is open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 4 PM, and closed on weekends. Call (559) 781-8000 or email management@sierraplaceapartments.com.

How do I schedule a tour?

Request a tour online and a member of our leasing staff will contact you by phone or email to confirm a time, or call (559) 781-8000 during office hours.

How do I apply?

Call (559) 781-8000 or send us your details, and a member of our staff will walk you through the application process. Every applicant receives a thorough background check, so allow time for screening in your moving plans.

Is Sierra Place close to Sierra View Medical Center?

Yes. Sierra View Medical Center is 0.7 miles away, about a 15-minute walk or a 3-minute drive. More for healthcare workers.

How far is Sierra Place from Visalia, Bakersfield and Fresno?

About 40 minutes to Visalia, 1 hour to Bakersfield and 1 hour 20 minutes to Fresno by car. Read our guide to moving to Porterville.

Which schools serve Sierra Place?

Sierra Place is in the Porterville Unified School District. Porterville High School is about a mile away, Monte Vista Elementary about 1.3 miles and Sequoia Middle School about 2.5 miles. School assignments can change, so confirm them with the district.

How do residents pay rent?

Residents can sign up for automatic payments from a checking account with no fee, or pay by credit card for a $9.99 fee per payment. See online payment options.

How do residents request maintenance?

Submit a work order online. For a maintenance emergency, call (559) 793-8723 at any time. Sierra Place has 24-hour emergency maintenance.
