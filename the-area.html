Living in Porterville

Porterville is a city of about 60,000 people in Tulare County, at the foot of the southern Sierra Nevada. Its largest employers are in healthcare, public services, education and distribution, and the city is a gateway to Lake Success, Sequoia National Forest and Giant Sequoia National Monument.
