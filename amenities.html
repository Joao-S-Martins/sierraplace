Apartment & Community Features at Sierra Place, Porterville, CA

Sierra Place offers one- and two-bedroom apartments with central air conditioning, an enclosed swimming pool and covered parking, in a quiet, landscaped community in central Porterville.

  • 1 Bedroom & 2 Bedroom Apartment Floor Plans

  • Central Air Conditioning
  • Covered Parking
