Moving to Porterville, CA

A Renter's Guide from Sierra Place Apartments

Starting a new job in Porterville, or moving from Bakersfield, Fresno or Visalia? This guide covers where Porterville is, who's hiring nearby, how long the drives are and how to tour Sierra Place from out of town.

Where is Porterville?

Porterville is a city of about 60,000 people in Tulare County, in California's southern San Joaquin Valley. It sits at the foot of the Sierra Nevada, about an hour north of Bakersfield and 40 minutes from Visalia, and it's a gateway to Lake Success, Sequoia National Forest and Giant Sequoia National Monument.

Moving from Bakersfield

Porterville is about an hour north of Bakersfield by Highway 65 and Highway 99, so trips back to see family and friends are easy. The Highway 65 on-ramp is less than a mile from Sierra Place. You'll also be closer to the mountains: Lake Success is about 20 minutes away, and Springville and Sequoia National Forest about 30.

Moving from Fresno

From Fresno, Porterville is about an hour and 20 minutes south. Fresno Yosemite International Airport is about an hour and a half from Sierra Place, and Visalia, Tulare County's largest city, is about 40 minutes away for shopping and services.

Moving from Visalia or Tulare

Porterville is 35 to 40 minutes from Visalia and Tulare. If you'll keep working there, for example at Kaweah Health Medical Center or Adventist Health Tulare, Sierra Place is less than a mile from Highway 65. If your new job is in Porterville, most major employers are within 15 minutes.

Who's hiring in Porterville

Porterville's largest employers are all within about 15 minutes of Sierra Place:

  • Sierra View Medical Center, a 167-bed acute care hospital, is 0.7 miles away. More for healthcare workers.
  • Porterville Developmental Center, run by the California Department of Developmental Services, is about 12 minutes away.
  • Walmart Distribution Center is about 10 minutes away.
  • Eagle Mountain Casino, which opened its larger location near Highway 65 in 2023, is about 15 minutes away.
  • Porterville College and Porterville Unified School District schools are 3 to 7 minutes away.

Getting to know Porterville

Downtown Porterville's Main Street is about 5 minutes from Sierra Place. The city hosts the Porterville Iris Festival each spring, and Lake Success offers boating, fishing and camping about 20 minutes away. For a bigger day trip, the Ash Mountain entrance to Sequoia National Park is a little over an hour's drive. See more commute times.
