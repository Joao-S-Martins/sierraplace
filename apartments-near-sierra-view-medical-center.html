Apartments Near Sierra View Medical Center

Sierra Place Apartments, 0.7 Miles from the Hospital in Porterville, CA

If you work at Sierra View Medical Center, Sierra Place is one of the closest apartment communities to the hospital: 0.7 miles from 465 W. Putnam Avenue, about a 15-minute walk or a 3-minute drive. Nurses, physicians, technicians, therapists and support staff spend less time commuting and more time resting between shifts.

Why healthcare workers like living here

  • Home fast after a long shift. The hospital is a few minutes away, so there's no cross-town drive after a 12-hour shift.
  • Covered parking. Keep your car shaded from the Central Valley sun, whether you work days or nights.
  • Central air conditioning for sleeping through hot summer days after a night shift.
  • A pool to unwind. Relax at the enclosed swimming pool and sundeck on your days off.
  • Peace of mind. Every applicant goes through a thorough background check.
  • 24-hour emergency maintenance, whatever your schedule.
  • Small to medium pets welcome.

Four floor plans

  • The Cedar: 1 bedroom, 1 bath, about 654 sq ft, with an oversized bathroom.
  • The Sierra: 2 bedrooms, 1 bath, about 859 sq ft, with an eat-in kitchen and a walk-in closet in the second bedroom.
  • The Sequoia: 2 bedrooms, 1.5 baths, about 1,110 sq ft, a two-story townhome-style plan with a private balcony.
  • The Redwood: 2 bedrooms, 2 baths, about 1,133 sq ft. Two full bathrooms make it a good fit for coworkers sharing an apartment.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Sierra Place from Sierra View Medical Center?

Sierra Place is 0.7 miles from Sierra View Medical Center at 465 W. Putnam Avenue in Porterville. That's about a 15-minute walk or a 3-minute drive.

Can I walk to work at the hospital?

Yes. The walk from Sierra Place to Sierra View Medical Center is about 15 minutes.

Do you have apartments for roommates?

Yes. The Redwood has two bedrooms and two full bathrooms, and the two-story Sequoia has two bedrooms upstairs with 1.5 baths, so each person gets their own space.

Do you offer leases for travel nurses and other short assignments?

Lease options depend on availability. Call Homes for Rent at (559) 781-8000, Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 4 PM, to ask what's available for your assignment dates.

Is parking covered?

Covered parking is available, which helps keep your car shaded in the Central Valley sun.
